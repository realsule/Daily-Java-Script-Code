About Us

Sipora started as a small stall with one blender and a single mojito recipe. Today we're a favourite stop in Kibera, Karanja Rd for fresh, alcohol-free mojitos, cold-pressed juices, thick milkshakes, and hand-glazed donuts — all made fresh, every single day. We believe great drinks and treats don't need shortcuts. No syrups from concentrate, no day-old donuts. Just real fruit, real cream, and real care in every order.
